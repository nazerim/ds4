The single governing factor is the **geological structure of the bedrock**, specifically its fractured nature. This is because the fractured bedrock acts as the primary reservoir and conduit for groundwater, sustaining baseflow through deep subsurface storage rather than shallow soil moisture; consequently, the stream's response to rainfall is decoupled from immediate precipitation intensity, as the water must first percolate through the soil and enter the fracture network, introducing a significant time lag and buffering effect that separates the hydrological response from the direct meteorological input.
