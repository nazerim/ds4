The single governing factor is the **geological structure of the bedrock**, specifically its **fractured nature**. This is because the fractured bedrock acts as the primary reservoir and conduit for groundwater, providing a stable, slow-release source of baseflow that is physically separated from the rapid, surface-driven runoff processes associated with soil moisture. Consequently, the stream's baseflow response is decoupled from immediate rainfall intensity, as the water must percolate through and be released from the deep, fractured rock matrix rather than responding directly to surface saturation conditions.
