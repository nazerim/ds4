The single governing factor is the **geological structure of the bedrock**, specifically its fractured nature. This is because the fractured bedrock acts as the primary reservoir and conduit for groundwater, sustaining baseflow through deep subsurface pathways rather than through shallow soil moisture. This geological characteristic physically decouples the stream's hydrological response from immediate rainfall intensity, as the water must percolate through and move along these rock fractures, resulting in a delayed and buffered streamflow response that is independent of surface soil saturation levels.
