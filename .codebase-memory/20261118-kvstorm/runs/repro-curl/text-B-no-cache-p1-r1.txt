The single governing factor is the **geological structure of the catchment**, specifically the presence of **fractured bedrock**. This factor dictates the hydrological behavior because it shifts the primary source of baseflow from shallow soil moisture to deep groundwater storage within rock fractures. Since water movement through fractured bedrock is governed by subsurface permeability and storage capacity rather than immediate surface saturation, the stream's response becomes decoupled from rainfall intensity, resulting in a delayed and sustained flow that is insensitive to short-term precipitation events.
